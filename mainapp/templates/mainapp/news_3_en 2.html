Uzbek producers aim to export over $100 million

{% endblock %} {% block content %}
The Ministry of Investment, Industry and Trade of the Republic of Uzbekistan, together with the international operator company IMGR, is implementing an export accelerator program for Uzbek manufacturers.

At the first stage, more than 500 applications were collected, from which 100 participants were selected - manufacturing companies from various industries intending to enter foreign markets.

The participants of the export accelerator program have set their sights on exports of more than $100 million in 2025.
