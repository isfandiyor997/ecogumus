What is vermicompost and what is its composition?

{% endblock header_content %} {% block content %}

Vermicompost is a product of processing organic matter by California Reds earthworms. It is a loose, finely granular mass. with granule sizes 1-3 mm, possibly up to 5mm. Concentrated fertilizer It contains, in a balanced combination, a whole range of nutrients substances - micro and macronutrients, mineral fertilizers, amino acids, humic acid, humates and fulvates of sodium and potassium, natural phytohormones, spores useful for plants of soil microorganisms, gibberellins in an easily digestible form.

Due to intensive fermentation, it is enhanced:
  1. Plant growth and development.
  2. Increases the germination and germination energy of seeds.
  3. It stimulates root formation in plants.
  4. Enhances plant immunity.
  5. Increases the fertility and looseness of the soil.
  6. It keeps moisture in the soil for a long time.
  7. Reduces the content of nitrites in agricultural products.
  8. It prevents the entry of heavy metals and radionuclides into the plant.
  9. Increases the content of sugars, proteins and vitamins in fruits and vegetables.
  10. Eliminates chlorosis, stimulates flowering and fruiting.
  11. It has the effect of rapid fruit ripening for 7-10 days.
  12. Stress is relieved, especially when planting plants and seeds.
  13. Products grown on vermicompost practically increase the shelf life.
  14. Vermicompost is 10 times higher than manure in terms of nutrient content. Vermicompost is more effective than manure by 10 times or more. And fresh manure It is a source of fungal and viral diseases of plants. Vermicompost completely lacks viable weed seeds, has antiseptic properties, destroying viral and fungal plant diseases.
The chemical composition of Vermicompost:
External appearance Homogeneous crumbly mass from dark brown to black in color. Inclusions of CDCs and their cocoons are allowed.
Mass fraction of organic substances, %, not less 55%
Mass fraction of moisture, % 40-45%
Mass fraction of ash, % 35-40%
Organic substances 55-65%
Mass fraction of Humus, % 28-45%
pH of 1% suspension 6.8-7.2%
Friability, % 100%
Total nitrogen N, % 1.5-4.8%
Total phosphorus (P2O5) %, not less 2.1-3.5%
Total potassium (K2O) %, not less 2.3-3.4%
Mass fraction of dry residue, %, not less 45%
Calcium 4.0-6.0%
Magnesium 0.6-2.3%
Iron 0.6-2.5%
Manganese 60-80 mg/k
Mass fraction of heavy metals, mg/kg below the maximum permissible concentration for soils
Pathogenic microflora absent
Helminth eggs absent
